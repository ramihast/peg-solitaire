How to Play

Goal

Remove pegs by jumping over them. Aim to finish with exactly one peg left.

Making a move

  • Tap or click a peg to select it. It lifts up and gets a gold ring with a ✓.
  • Holes you can jump into show a green dashed ring with a dot. The peg you would jump over shows a ×.
  • Tap a highlighted hole to jump. The peg you jumped over is removed.
  • You can also drag a peg onto a highlighted hole.
  • Jumps go straight up, down, left or right over one neighbouring peg into an empty hole. Diagonal jumps are not allowed.
  • Tap the selected peg again, or press Esc, to cancel the selection.

Buttons

  • Undo / Redo: step backward or forward through your moves. Making a new move after Undo clears the Redo history.
  • Hint: shows one legal move with a blue pulsing ring. Press it again to see a different move.
  • Restart: puts the current game back at its starting position.
  • New Game: starts a fresh standard game and clears all history.

Keyboard

  • Tab moves focus into the board. The ← ↑ → ↓ arrow keys move between holes.
  • Enter or Space selects a peg or jumps into a highlighted hole.
  • Esc cancels a selection or closes a dialog.

End of the game

When no legal jumps are left, the game ends and you see your result. One peg left is a win.
